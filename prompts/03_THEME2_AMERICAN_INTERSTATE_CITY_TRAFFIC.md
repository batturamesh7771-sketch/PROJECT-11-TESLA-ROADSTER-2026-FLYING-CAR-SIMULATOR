# Prompt 03: Theme 2 — Heavy City Traffic Escape Trial & American Expressway

## Mission 09 Specification
Build a brand-new, switchable Theme 2 proving ground without modifying or breaking Theme 1 (McGregor Aerobatic Ramp Loop).

### Requirements:
1. **Isolated Scene Architecture:**
   - Encapsulate Theme 1 inside `theme1Group` and Theme 2 inside `theme2Group`.
   - Toggle visibility and physics solvers cleanly via top-level mode selector.

2. **Authentic 5-Lane American Urban Interstate Freeway:**
   - Realistic multi-lane layout (I-10 / I-405 Downtown Expressway style).
   - High-density procedural traffic fleet:
     * Stalled semi-trucks with 4.2m box trailers.
     * Cybertrucks, SUVs, sedans, yellow taxicabs, and highway patrol units.
   - Overhead highway signage gantries, concrete Jersey barriers, and high-mast street illumination.
   - Downtown skyline with 48 illuminated high-rise towers and dusk twilight atmosphere.

3. **Autonomous Traffic Escape Flight Sequence (5 Stages):**
   - **Stage 1 (Gridlock Detection):** Car approaches stalled bumper-to-bumper queue and slows.
   - **Stage 2 (Vertical COPV Liftoff):** 10-nozzle cold-gas vertical jump to 10.5m clearance with dust blast ring.
   - **Stage 3 (Transonic Flyover):** 95 km/h horizontal cruise directly over stalled 4.2m semi-trailers with 360° flat kickflip.
   - **Stage 4 (Retro-Catch Deceleration):** Diffuser and forward nozzles fire counter-thrust to arrest forward velocity.
   - **Stage 5 (Highway Touchdown):** Smooth landing in open highway lane ahead of gridlock, resuming ground wheel drive.
